✦ Our Story

Baked with
intention,
not just ingredients.

AbbioccoBakes was born out of a simple belief — that a beautiful cake should taste every bit as good as it looks. Every creation is slow-crafted by Muneeba from her Karachi kitchen, using recipes refined over time and flavours chosen with care.

No shortcuts, no compromises. From Black & Gold fault-line graduation cakes to hand-painted baby shower cakesicles and walnut fudge brownies — everything is made-to-order, just for you.

"The detailing was so neat — they matched my theme perfectly and tasted just as good as they looked."
— Verified customer review, Instagram
